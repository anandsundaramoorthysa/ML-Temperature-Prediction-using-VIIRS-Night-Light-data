Variable Descriptions

Variable Description Type
Year Temporal variable indicating the observation year (2014-2024) Integer
Month Seasonal variable indicating the month (1-12). Captures intra-annual temperature patterns. Integer
NightLight VIIRS nighttime light intensity (proxy for human activity). Higher values indicate more human activity, population density, and economic activity. Monthly resolution captures short-term patterns. Float
Avg_Monthly_Temperature Average monthly temperature in degrees Celsius. Calculated by aggregating hourly/daily temperature readings for each month. Float
Why Nightlights as Urbanization Proxy?

VIIRS (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite) nighttime light intensity is a widely-used proxy for urbanization because:

  • Urban areas produce significantly more nighttime light than rural areas
  • Light intensity correlates with population density and economic activity
  • Provides consistent, remotely-sensed data across large areas
  • Complements traditional census-based urbanization metrics

Note: Nightlights are a proxy, not a direct measure. Other factors (industrial activity, energy policies) may also influence light intensity.
