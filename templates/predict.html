Interpretation Guidelines

Year

Long-term temporal trends (2014-2024). May include global warming effects and urbanization growth.

Month

Seasonal variable (1-12) capturing intra-annual temperature patterns in Chennai's tropical climate.

Nightlight Intensity

Monthly human activity proxy. Higher values indicate more activity, population, and economic development.

Prediction

Predicted average monthly temperature (°C) for the specified year-month combination with given nightlight level.

Note: Predictions are based on monthly historical patterns (2014-2024) and may not account for future changes in climate, urbanization patterns, or other factors. Monthly resolution captures seasonal variations that yearly data cannot.
